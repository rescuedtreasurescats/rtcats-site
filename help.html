Google says it can't open the Scheduling Portal

  1. Always open the Scheduling Portal from RTCats HQ. Avoid old bookmarks or saved text messages.
  2. If you have multiple Google accounts signed in, remove the extra account(s) and try again.
  3. On an iPhone or iPad, open the portal in a Private Safari window.
  4. If you're opening the portal from Facebook Messenger, use Open in Safari instead of Messenger's built-in browser.
  5. Try Chrome if Safari still won't open the portal.
  6. If it still fails, send Sue a screenshot showing the error and the address bar.

Common iPhone Issues

  1. Open links in Safari. If you opened the portal from Facebook or Messenger, tap the Share button and choose Open in Safari.
  2. Use a Private Safari tab.
    Open Safari → Tap the Tabs button → Tap Private → Tap the + to open a new Private tab → Open RTCats HQ and try again.
  3. If Safari still won't work, install Chrome. Open the portal in Chrome instead.
  4. Restart Safari. Close all Safari windows and reopen the browser.
  5. Restart your iPhone. A simple restart fixes many browser issues.
  6. Update iOS. Older versions of Safari occasionally have compatibility issues.

Multiple Google Accounts

  1. If Google says it cannot open the Scheduling Portal, the most common cause is having multiple Google accounts signed in.
  2. Open gmail.com and tap your profile picture.
  3. If you see more than one Google account, temporarily remove the extra account(s) and try again.
  4. After removing the extra account, reopen the portal from RTCats HQ.
  5. If you need both accounts, you can usually add the second account back after the portal is working.
  6. If Google keeps changing the web address to something containing /u/1/ or /u/2/, this is almost always a Google account issue.

Messenger Browser Issues

  1. Facebook Messenger opens links inside its own browser instead of Safari or Chrome.
  2. Some Google pages do not work correctly inside Messenger.
  3. Tap the menu in Messenger and choose Open in Safari (or Chrome).
  4. If that option isn't available, copy the link and paste it directly into Safari.
  5. Avoid saving Messenger versions of links as bookmarks.
